Supported Firmwares

These custom firmwares (CFWs) are actively supported by PortMaster:

Firmware Notes
ArkOS Fully supported. Install script goes in /roms/tools/.
AmberELEC Fully supported.
ROCKNIX Fully supported. The spiritual successor to AmberELEC.
muOS Fully supported.
Knulli Fully supported.
uOS / UnofficialOS Partially supported.
dArkOS Fully supported.

Unsupported Firmwares

These firmwares are no longer supported by PortMaster. They may have worked in the past but are discontinued or no longer maintained:

Firmware Reason
JELOS Discontinued. The project is no longer maintained. Users are encouraged to migrate to ROCKNIX, which is the active continuation of JELOS.
